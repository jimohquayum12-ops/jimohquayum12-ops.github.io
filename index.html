How to Free Up Storage

Open your phone's Settings and look for Storage. Review large files, unused apps and downloads. Keep important photos and documents backed up before deleting anything.

How to Improve Battery Life

Check Battery settings to see which apps use the most power. Lowering screen brightness, reducing unnecessary background activity and keeping Android updated can help.

What to Do When Your Phone Is Slow

Restart the phone, close apps you no longer need, check available storage and install system updates. Avoid installing unknown “phone booster” apps.

Protect Your Android Phone

Use a strong screen lock, install updates, review app permissions and download apps from trusted sources. Turn on device-finding features where available.

Fix Common Wi-Fi Problems

Check that Wi-Fi is enabled, restart the connection, restart your router if you have access to it, and make sure your phone is connected to the correct network.

Useful Android Settings

Explore Privacy, Notifications, Battery, Accessibility and Security settings. Menu names can differ depending on your Android version and phone manufacturer.
